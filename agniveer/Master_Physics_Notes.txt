================================================================================
CHAPTER 1 - PHYSICAL WORLD, UNITS AND MEASUREMENT
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION

Physics starts with MEASUREMENT. Jab bhi koi quantity likhte hain — jaise "speed 20 m/s"
— usme do cheezein hoti hain: ek NUMBER aur ek UNIT. Is chapter mein hum seekhte hain ki
units kya hain, dimensions kya hain, aur dono ka use karke exam questions kaise turant
solve hote hain. Ye chapter sabse zyada direct-scoring hai: dimensions aur unit
conversion ke questions har paper mein aate hain.
2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What is a physical quantity? Koi bhi cheez jo measure ki ja sake (length, mass, time,
  temperature, current) physical quantity hai. Ek UNIT (kg, metre, second) khud
  quantity NAHI hoti — kg mass ka unit hai, mass quantity hai. Yehi ek classic trap
  hai.
- Fundamental vs derived. SI system mein 7 BASE units hain: length (m), mass (kg), time
  (s), electric current (A), temperature (K), amount of substance (mol), luminous
  intensity (cd). Baaki sab quantities DERIVED hain — force, work, power sab inhi 7 se
  banti hain.
- What is a dimensional formula? Har quantity ko base dimensions [M^a L^b T^c A^d] mein
  express karna. Mass (M), Length (L), Time (T), Current (A). Example: velocity =
  displacement/time, isliye [velocity] = [L]/[T] = [M⁰L¹T⁻¹].
- Homogeneity principle. Kisi bhi physical equation mein HAR term ka dimension same hona
  chahiye. Isi se hum unknown constant ka dimension nikalte hain — bas equation ke
  kisi bhi do same-dimension terms ko equate kar do.
3. KEY RELATIONS

- Dimension of a constant: us constant ko equation se alag karo, phir dimensions
  substitute karo. Example: v = at + bt² mein har term ka dimension velocity ka hai,
  isliye [a] = [v/t] = LT⁻² aur [b] = [v/t²] = LT⁻³.
- Same-dimension pairs (yaad rakhne wale twins): torque = work = energy; stress =
  pressure = Young's modulus; momentum = impulse; angular momentum = Planck's
  constant.
- Dimensionless BUT with units: angle (radian), solid angle (steradian). Ye number hote
  hain phir bhi unit rakhte hain.
- Unitless AND dimensionless: strain, refractive index, specific gravity, relative
  permittivity, Poisson ratio, magnification, power factor — sab pure ratios hain.
4. FORMULAS

- [velocity] = [M⁰L¹T⁻¹]   [acceleration] = [M⁰L¹T⁻²]
- [force] = [M¹L¹T⁻²]   [pressure] = [stress] = [M¹L⁻¹T⁻²]
- [energy] = [work] = [torque] = [M¹L²T⁻²]   [power] = [M¹L²T⁻³]
- [momentum] = [impulse] = [M¹L¹T⁻¹]   [angular momentum] = [Planck's h] = [M¹L²T⁻¹]
- [viscosity] = [M¹L⁻¹T⁻¹]   [resistivity] = [M¹L³T⁻³A⁻²]   [G] = [M⁻¹L³T⁻²]
- 1 kWh = 3.6×10⁶ J ; 1 N = 10⁵ dyne ; 1 T = 10⁴ gauss ; 1 pm = 10⁻¹² m = 10⁻⁶ micron
- °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9 ; °F = (9/5)°C + 32 ; 1 cal = 4.18 J
- σ (Stefan) = 5.67 × 10⁻⁸ W m⁻² K⁻⁴ ; pole strength unit = A·m
5. CONDITIONS / SPECIAL CASES

- 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J (kilo-watt-hour → joules).
- [potential] = [M¹L²T⁻³A⁻¹] ; [magnetic field B] = [M¹T⁻²A⁻¹] (F = qvB se).
- Coefficient of viscosity η ka SI unit Pa·s (poiseuille), CGS unit poise.
- Time (second) hi ek aisi quantity hai jiska unit CGS aur SI dono mein SAME hai.
- km/h → m/s: ×5/18 (54 km/h = 15 m/s).
- Light-year = light dwara vacuum mein 1 saal mein tay ki gayi doori = unit of DISTANCE
  (time nahi!). 1 ly ≈ 9.46 × 10¹⁵ m.
- 'nit' = unit of luminance (photometric quantity).
- Density unit-conversion: 128 kg/m³ ko aise system mein badlo jahan mass unit 50 g aur
  length unit 25 cm ho → 128 × (1000/50) × (0.25/1)³ = 40.
- °F ↔ °C: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9 ; °F = (9/5)°C + 32. 200 °F → (200−32) × 5/9 = 93.3 °C.
- Stefan–Boltzmann constant σ = 5.67 × 10⁻⁸ W m⁻² K⁻⁴ (radiated energy ∝ T⁴).
- Pole strength ka unit A·m (magnetic dipole moment ka unit A·m²).
- Mechanical equivalent of heat: 1 cal = 4.18 J.
- CGS unit of electric dipole moment = statC·cm.
6. HOW TO RECOGNIZE THE QUESTION

- "Dimension of X find karo" → defining formula lo, dimensions substitute karo.
- "Kaunsa pair same dimension rakhta hai?" → dono ko formula se likho, compare karo.
- "Kaun dimensionless/unitless hai?" → strain, refractive index, specific gravity,
angle.
- "Unit conversion" (kWh→J, N→dyne, T→gauss, pm→micron) → conversion ladder yaad rakho.
- "Fundamental vs derived?" → 7 base SI units se match karo.
7. SOLVING METHODS

- Dimension of constant: equation se constant ko symbolically alag karo, phir substitute
  karo.
- Energy/Volume ka dimension PRESSURE ke barabar hota hai — ye equivalence exam mein
  directly aata hai.
- Density/unit conversions ke liye ratio formula: new value = old × (old mass unit/new
  mass unit) × (new length unit/old length unit)³.
8. REAL PYQ EXAMPLES

Q. If v = at + bt², v is velocity and t is in seconds, then the dimension of b is:
   Method: Homogeneity principle — har term ka dimension velocity (LT⁻¹) ke barabar hota hai.
   Solve: [b] × [t²] = [v] → [b] = [v]/[t²] = LT⁻¹ / T² = LT⁻³.
   Takeaway: Kaunse power mein time aata hai, usi se exponent decide hota hai. b ka dimension LT⁻³.

Q. The dimensional formula for specific gravity is:
   Method: Specific gravity = density/density — ek pure ratio.
   Solve: Ratio mein sab kuch cancel ho jaata hai → [M⁰L⁰T⁰].
   Takeaway: Koi bhi pure ratio (strain, refractive index, specific gravity) dimensionless hota hai.

Q. A non-SI unit called 'nit' is the unit of which photometric quantity?
   Method: Units ke facts — 'nit' luminance ka unit hai.
   Solve: Luminance = light intensity per area; iska unit nit hai.
   Takeaway: Direct fact. Nit = luminance. Lumen = luminous flux (don't mix).

Q. A measured temperature is 200 °F. Its value in Celsius is:
   Method: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9.
   Solve: °C = (200 − 32) × 5/9 = 168 × 5/9 = 93.3 °C.
   Takeaway: Pehle 32 ghato, phir 5/9 se multiply. °C → °F iska ulta (×9/5 + 32).

9. 10-SECOND TRICKS

- Jo cheez sirf number hai (strain, refractive index, specific gravity, angle) →
  dimensionless. MCQ mein turant eliminate karo.
- Planck's constant aur angular momentum hamesha twins hain.
- °F → °C: (F − 32) × 5/9 ; 200 °F = 93.3 °C.
- 1 cal = 4.18 J (heat ↔ mechanical work ka bridge).
- "kg" ek unit hai (quantity nahi); "current (A)" base quantity hai; "frequency (Hz)"
  derived hai — classic elimination points.
10. IMPORTANT FACTS

- 7 base SI units: metre, kilogram, second, ampere, kelvin, mole, candela.
- Frequency ka unit Hz = s⁻¹ (derived).
- Permittivity ka unit: C²N⁻¹m⁻². Permeability μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ T m/A.
11. COMMON TRAPS

TRAP: "kg" ek physical quantity hai.
FIX:  kg ek UNIT hai; mass quantity hai. Ye trap bar-bar aata hai.
TRAP: Specific gravity/strain ka "koi unit nahi hai to koi dimension nahi" — galat way mein.
FIX:  Ye dimensionless hain: dimension [M⁰L⁰T⁰] hota hai (zero nahi hota, zero-POWER hota hai).
TRAP: Angle ka koi unit nahi hota.
FIX:  Angle ka unit RADIAN hota hai, lekin dimension nahi hota.
12. GRAPHS / DIAGRAMS

- Dimensions ka table hi is chapter ka "diagram" hai — FORMULAS section ki table yaad
  karo.
- Conversion ladder: 1 kWh = 3.6×10⁶ J ; 1 N = 10⁵ dyne ; 1 T = 10⁴ gauss ; 1 pm = 10⁻⁶
  micron.
13. LAST-MINUTE REVISION

- LAST-MINUTE REVISION
- -[Energy] = [Work] = [Torque] = [ML²T⁻²] ; [Power] = [ML²T⁻³]
- -[Pressure] = [Stress] = [Young's modulus] = [ML⁻¹T⁻²] = [Energy/Volume]
- -[Momentum] = [Impulse] = [MLT⁻¹] ; [Angular momentum] = [h] = [ML²T⁻¹]
- -[Viscosity] = [ML⁻¹T⁻¹] ; [Resistivity] = [ML³T⁻³A⁻²]
- -1 kWh = 3.6×10⁶ J ; 1 N = 10⁵ dyne ; 1 T = 10⁴ gauss ; 1 pm = 10⁻⁶ micron

================================================================================
CHAPTER 2 - KINEMATICS
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION

Kinematics = motion ki study bina ye pooche ki motion kisne kiya. Yahan sirf position,
velocity, acceleration aur time ka relation padhte hain. Ye chapter har exam ka backbone
hai kyunki yahi se equations of motion, graphs aur projectile ke questions aate hain.
2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What is displacement vs distance? Displacement ek VECTOR hai (direction ke saath),
  distance ek SCALAR hai. Closed loop mein ghoom kar wapas aane par displacement = 0,
  lekin distance > 0. Isliye average velocity = NET displacement/time (closed path
  mein zero), jabki average speed = total distance/time.
- What is acceleration? Acceleration batata hai ki velocity kitni jaldi badal rahi hai.
  Agar har second velocity same amount se badle, acceleration constant hota hai, aur
  tab hum uniform-acceleration equations use kar sakte hain. a = dv/dt.
- What is a projectile? Ek body jise kisi angle par upar phenka gaya ho. Uski horizontal
  velocity constant rehti hai (koi horizontal force nahi), vertical motion free fall
  hai (g neeche). In dono ko alag-alag treat karo.
- Motion graphs. x-t graph ka SLOPE = velocity; v-t graph ka SLOPE = acceleration; v-t
  graph ka AREA = displacement; a-t graph ka AREA = velocity change; F-t graph ka AREA
  = impulse.
3. KEY RELATIONS

- Three equations (constant acceleration): v = u + at ; s = ut + (1/2)at² ; v² = u² +
  2as.
- Distance in nth second: Sₙ = u + (a/2)(2n − 1).
- Free fall (u = 0, a = g): h = (1/2)gt² ; v = √(2gh) ; t = √(2h/g).
- Per-second distance ratio in free fall = 1 : 3 : 5 : 7 (odd numbers).
- Total distance ratio in 1,2,3,4 seconds = 1 : 4 : 9 : 16 (squares).
- Projectile: H = u²sin²θ/2g ; R = u²sin²θ/g ; T = 2u sinθ/g ; max range at θ = 45°.
- Circular motion: a = v²/r = ω²r ; v = ωr ; ω = 2π/T.
4. FORMULAS

- v = u + at ; s = ut + (1/2)at² ; v² = u² + 2as ; s = ((u+v)/2)t
- Sₙ = u + (a/2)(2n − 1)
- a = dv/dt = v(dv/dx) ; a(centripetal) = v²/r = ω²r
- H = u²sin²θ/2g ; R = u²sin²θ/g ; T = 2u sinθ/g
Symbol meanings: u = initial velocity, v = final velocity, a = acceleration, s =
displacement, t = time, θ = projection angle, g = 9.8 m/s², r = radius, ω = angular
speed.
5. CONDITIONS / SPECIAL CASES

- Body starts from rest → u = 0, to s = (1/2)at². "Car from rest to 20 m/s in 10 s": a =
  2 m/s², s = (1/2)(2)(100) = 100 m.
- Projectile: max range 45° par; complementary angles (30°+60°, 20°+70°) par equal
  range.
- Highest point par projectile ki velocity sirf horizontal (u cosθ) hoti hai;
  acceleration g hi rehti hai (kabhi zero nahi).
- s = xt² − yt + z (polynomial in t): a = d²s/dt² = 2x — CONSTANT, isliye t = 4 s par
  bhi 2x hi hoga.
- Average speed (distance segments): v_avg = total distance / total time. 1/3 distance
  at 60 km/h, 2/3 at 30 km/h → v_avg = 36 km/h.
- Escalator/moving walkway: combined time t = t₁t₂/(t₁+t₂) (walk speed + walkway speed).
- Coefficient of restitution e = separation speed / approach speed; bounce ke baad
  height = e²h (perfectly elastic e = 1).
- Circular motion mein velocity double → acceleration 4× (a ∝ v²). Ratio after:before =
  4:1.
6. HOW TO RECOGNIZE THE QUESTION

- "nth second / per-second ratio" → Sₙ = u + (a/2)(2n−1); odd ratio 1:3:5:7.
- "Slope/area of a graph" → slope x-t = v, slope v-t = a, area v-t = s.
- "Projectile range/height/time" → H, R, T formulas.
- "Uniform circular motion" → a = v²/r = ω²r.
- "s as polynomial in t" → differentiate TWICE for acceleration.
7. SOLVING METHODS

- Constant-acceleration question: list karo u, v, a, s, t; wahi equation lo jisme knowns
  + unknown hon.
- Ratio problems (heights h, 2h): time ∝ √h → ratio √1 : √2.
- Graph questions: slope ya area se quantity identify karo (curve ki shape se nahi).
8. REAL PYQ EXAMPLES

Q. The ratio of the distances travelled by a freely falling body in the 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th second:
   Method: Distance in nth second, u = 0 (free fall).
   Solve: Sₙ = u + (a/2)(2n−1) = 0 + (g/2)(2n−1) → Sₙ ∝ (2n−1).
       n = 1,2,3,4 → 2n−1 = 1, 3, 5, 7.
   Takeaway: Free-fall per-second distances hamesha odd numbers 1:3:5:7.

Q. A car accelerates uniformly from rest to 20 m/s in 10 seconds. How far does it travel?
   Method: Uniform acceleration, u = 0, v = 20, t = 10.
   Solve: s = ((u+v)/2) × t = (0+20)/2 × 10 = 100 m.
   Faster: Average-velocity method: v_avg = (u+v)/2 = 10 m/s → s = 10 × 10 = 100 m.
   Takeaway: Average velocity × time is the fastest route for uniform acceleration.

Q. Two objects are dropped from heights h and 2h. Ratio of times to reach the ground:
   Method: Free fall: t = √(2h/g), so t ∝ √h.
   Solve: t₁:t₂ = √h : √(2h) = 1 : √2.
   Takeaway: Time ∝ √height. Double height → time ×√2 (not double).

9. 10-SECOND TRICKS

- Odd-number trick: 1:3:5:7 ALWAYS free-fall per-second ratio.
- Double velocity in circular motion → acceleration ×4.
- Ball up then caught → total time = 2u/g.
- Slope = derivative, area = integral — har motion graph par apply karo.
10. IMPORTANT FACTS

- Retardation = negative acceleration (deceleration).
11. COMMON TRAPS

TRAP: Average speed aur average velocity same hoti hai.
FIX:  Average velocity = NET displacement/time (closed loop → zero); average speed = total distance/time.
TRAP: s = xt² − yt + z mein t = 4 s par alag acceleration hota hai.
FIX:  a = 2x (constant) — t par depend nahi karta. Ye trap baar-baar aata hai.
TRAP: Retardation nikalne ke liye direct a = dv/dt lagao bina v = 0 wala time nikale.
FIX:  Pehle v = 0 se time nikalo, phir a = dv/dt. (x = 8 + 12t − t³: v = 12 − 3t² = 0 → t = 2 s → a = −6t = −12 → retardation 12 m/s².)
12. GRAPHS / DIAGRAMS

- x-t graph: slope = velocity; straight line = uniform velocity.
- v-t graph: slope = acceleration; AREA = displacement.
- a-t graph: area = change in velocity.
- Projectile path ek PARABOLA hai; range 45° par maximum.
13. LAST-MINUTE REVISION

- LAST-MINUTE REVISION
- -v = u + at ; s = ut + (1/2)at² ; v² = u² + 2as ; Sₙ = u + (a/2)(2n−1)
- -Free fall: per-second 1:3:5:7 ; total 1:4:9:16 ; t ∝ √h
- -Circular: a = v²/r = ω²r ; double v → 4× a
- -Projectile: H = u²sin²θ/2g ; R = u²sin²θ/g ; T = 2u sinθ/g

CHAPTER 3 - LAWS OF MOTION
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION

Ye chapter force aur motion ke beech ka connection samjhata hai. Newton ke teen laws,
momentum, impulse aur friction — sab yahin aate hain. Inka use hi baad mein work-energy
aur rotational motion ka base banta hai.
2. CORE CONCEPTS

- Newton's 1st law (inertia). Body apni state (rest ya uniform motion) tab tak nahi
  badalti jab tak net external force na lage. Mass hi inertia ka measure hai. Bus
  suddenly start ho to passenger peeche girta hai — ye INERTIA hai (1st law), action-
  reaction nahi.
- Newton's 2nd law. F = ma = dp/dt. Force = momentum ke change ki rate. Yahi
  quantitative law hai: force se acceleration, ya mass, ya momentum change sab nikalte
  hain.
- Newton's 3rd law. Har action ka equal aur opposite reaction. Boat se jump karo to boat
  peeche jaati hai — ye action-reaction hai. Ye law SAB interacting bodies par lagta
  hai.
- What is momentum? p = mv (vector). Isolated system (no external force) mein total
  momentum conserved rehta hai — isi se recoil problems solve hoti hain.
- What is impulse? J = F × Δt = change in momentum (Δp). Same momentum change ke liye:
  jitna LAMBA time, utna CHOTA force. Isi liye airbag, cushioned bed — stopping time
  badha kar force kam karte hain.
- What is equilibrium? Body par net force ZERO (translational) aur net torque ZERO
  (rotational) ho to body equilibrium mein hai. Zero resultant banane ke liye MINIMUM
  2 forces chahiye — equal aur opposite (1 force kabhi zero nahi de sakta, 3-4 zaroori
  nahi).
- What is a resultant? Do vectors A aur B ka resultant R = √(A² + B² + 2AB cosθ).
  Perpendicular (θ = 90°) → R = √(A²+B²); same direction → A+B; opposite → |A−B|.
- What is an Atwood machine? Pulley par do masses m₁, m₂ (m₂ > m₁) hang ho →
  acceleration a = (m₂−m₁)g/(m₁+m₂). Massless string hai to tension dono sides par
  SAME.
- What is apparent weight in a lift? Lift upar accelerate → R = m(g+a) (feel heavy).
  Neeche accelerate → R = m(g−a). Free fall (a = g) → R = 0 (weightless).
- Which forces are NOT electromagnetic? Tension, friction, normal, spring — sab
  electromagnetic hain (atom-atom charge forces). GRAVITY aur NUCLEAR force
  electromagnetic NAHI hain.
3. KEY RELATIONS

- F = ma ; p = mv ; J = FΔt = Δp.
- Kinetic energy vs momentum: K = p²/2m. K 4× ho jaye → p 2× (√4), NOT 4×.
- Momentum conserve: m₁v₁ = m₂v₂ → recoil v = (m × v)/M.
- Static friction max: f_max = μₛN ; bina slip ke max acceleration a = μₛg.
- Resultant: R = √(A² + B² + 2AB cosθ) ; ⊥ → √(A²+B²) → 3 kN & 4 kN → 5 kN.
- Atwood: a = (m₂−m₁)g/(m₁+m₂) ; lift: R = m(g ± a).
- Angle between vectors: cosθ = (A·B)/(|A||B|).
- Minimum forces for zero resultant = 2 (equal, opposite).
- Force from a, mass: F = ma (bus 8 tonnes, a = 0.5 → F = 4000 N).
4. FORMULAS

- F = ma = dp/dt ; p = mv ; J = FΔt = Δp
- f_max = μₛN ; a = μₛg
- K = p²/2m → p = √(2mK)
- R = √(A² + B² + 2AB cosθ) ; cosθ = (A·B)/(|A||B|)
- a_atwood = (m₂−m₁)g/(m₁+m₂) ; R_lift = m(g ± a)
Symbol meanings: F = force (N), m = mass (kg), a = acceleration, p = momentum, μₛ =
coefficient of static friction, N = normal force, J = impulse.
5. CONDITIONS / SPECIAL CASES

- Momentum double (mass fixed) → velocity double. 3000 units → 6000 units.
- 4× KE → 2× momentum. (K = p²/2m ka √ lete hain.)
- Airbag/cushion → time badhao → force kam. Hamesha "increase time" wala option.
- Recoil speed = (chhota mass × uski velocity)/bada mass. Bullet 100 g at 100 m/s, gun
  20 kg → v = (0.1×100)/20 = 0.5 m/s.
- 3 kN aur 4 kN ⊥ → resultant 5 kN (3-4-5 triangle).
- Momentum 25% badhao (p → 1.25p) → K = p²/2m → K ×(1.25)² = 1.5625 → 56.25% increment.
- Atwood: heavier side neeche, a = (m₂−m₁)g/(m₁+m₂).
- Lift upar → R = m(g+a); neeche → R = m(g−a).
6. HOW TO RECOGNIZE THE QUESTION

- "Force / mass / acceleration" → F = ma.
- "Momentum double / velocity double" → p = mv.
- "Recoil of a gun" → momentum before = after = 0.
- "Impulse / impact force" → J = FΔt = Δp (longer time → smaller force).
- "Max acceleration without slipping" → a = μₛg.
- "Resultant of two forces" → R = √(A²+B²+2AB cosθ).
- "Zero resultant minimum forces" → 2.
- "Pulley / two masses" → Atwood a = (m₂−m₁)g/(m₁+m₂).
- "Weight in a lift" → m(g ± a).
7. SOLVING METHODS

- Recoil: total momentum before = after (usually 0).
- F = ma directly for force/mass.
- Impulse: J = Ft = Δp.
- Friction threshold: F ≤ μₛN for rest; slipping starts at a = μₛg.
8. REAL PYQ EXAMPLES

Q. A bullet of mass 100 g is fired from a gun of mass 20 kg with a velocity of 100 m/s. The recoil velocity of the gun is:
   Method: Conservation of linear momentum (initially at rest).
   Solve: 0 = (0.1)(100) + (20)(v) → v = −10/20 = −0.5 m/s (minus = opposite direction).
   Takeaway: Recoil = (m × v)/M — chhota mass × velocity, bade mass se divide.

Q. An unbalanced force of 90 N produces an acceleration of 15 m/s². The mass is:
   Method: Newton's 2nd law directly.
   Solve: m = F/a = 90/15 = 6 kg.
   Takeaway: m = F/a — force aur acceleration se mass ek step mein.

Q. A bus of mass 8 tonnes rolls down a hill with acceleration 0.5 m/s². The force is:
   Method: F = ma with unit conversion (8 tonnes = 8000 kg).
   Solve: F = 8000 × 0.5 = 4000 N.
   Takeaway: Tonnes → kg pehle convert karo (×1000).

Q. A ball has 3000 units of momentum. If velocity is doubled, new momentum is:
   Method: p ∝ v (mass fixed).
   Solve: Double v → double p → 6000 units.
   Takeaway: Momentum ∝ velocity (mass constant). KE ∝ v² — dono confuse mat karo.

Q. A particle's linear momentum is increased by 25%. The percentage increase in kinetic energy is:
   Method: K = p²/2m — momentum ka factor square hota hai.
   Solve: p → 1.25p → K ∝ (1.25)² = 1.5625 → 56.25% increase.
   Takeaway: p ×n → K ×n². 1.25² = 1.5625 (56.25% up).

Q. Two forces of 3 kN and 4 kN act perpendicular to each other. Their resultant is:
   Method: Perpendicular vectors → Pythagoras.
   Solve: R = √(3² + 4²) = 5 kN.
   Takeaway: 3-4-5 right triangle → resultant 5 kN.

Q. Two masses m₁ and m₂ (m₂ > m₁) hang from a frictionless pulley. The acceleration is:
   Method: Atwood machine formula.
   Solve: a = (m₂ − m₁)g/(m₁ + m₂).
   Takeaway: Net force (m₂−m₁)g ko total mass (m₁+m₂) se divide karo.

9. 10-SECOND TRICKS

- Momentum double → velocity double.
- K n-times → p √n-times.
- Longer stopping time = smaller force (airbag). Always pick "increase time".
- Recoil speed = (small mass × velocity)/big mass.
- 3 kN ⊥ 4 kN → 5 kN ; p ×1.25 → K ×1.5625.
- Zero resultant ke liye minimum 2 forces (equal-opposite).
10. IMPORTANT FACTS

- Force ek vector hai; friction hamesha relative motion oppose karti hai.
- Newton's 3rd law SAB interacting bodies par lagta hai (boat-jump, recoil, rocket).
11. COMMON TRAPS

TRAP: Bus suddenly start → passenger peeche girta hai = Newton's 3rd law.
FIX:  Ye INERTIA hai (1st law). Boat se jump → boat peeche = 3rd law (action-reaction).
TRAP: 4× KE → 4× momentum.
FIX:  K = p²/2m se p = √(2mK) → 4× KE = 2× momentum.
TRAP: Airbag force badhata hai.
FIX:  Airbag stopping TIME badhata hai → force GHATTI hai (J = FΔt).
TRAP: Zero resultant ke liye 1 ya 3 forces kaafi hain.
FIX:  Minimum 2 forces (equal aur opposite). 1 force kabhi zero resultant nahi deta.
TRAP: Momentum 25% badhao to KE bhi 25% badhti hai.
FIX:  K ∝ p² → (1.25)² = 1.5625 → 56.25% increase.
12. GRAPHS / DIAGRAMS

- Free-body diagram: pehle weight, normal, friction, applied force draw karo.
- Force-time graph: area = impulse = momentum change.
- Momentum conserved with changing mass → v ∝ 1/m (hyperbola).
- Friction vs applied force: static friction μₛN tak badhta hai, phir kinetic μₖN par
  gir jaata hai.
13. LAST-MINUTE REVISION

- LAST-MINUTE REVISION
- -F = ma = dp/dt ; p = mv ; J = FΔt = Δp
- -f_max = μₛN ; slipping threshold a = μₛg
- -Recoil: v = (m × v)/M ; K = p²/2m → K×4 ⇒ p×2

CHAPTER 4 - WORK, ENERGY AND POWER
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION

Force ka motion par effect "work" ke through measure hota hai. Work-energy theorem hi
aage ke saare chapters (potential, kinetic, springs, power) ki neev hai. Ye chapter
short hai lekin highly scoring — dot product, spring energy aur power ke fixed question
types hain.
2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What is work? W = F·s = F s cosθ. Force ka displacement ki direction mein component hi
  kaam karta hai. Work ek SCALAR hai aur NEGATIVE bhi ho sakta hai — jab force motion
  oppose kare (retarding force → negative work).
- What is energy? Kaam karne ki capacity. Kinetic energy K = (1/2)mv² (motion ki
  energy), potential energy U = mgh (height ki) ya (1/2)kx² (spring ki).
- What is power? Kaam karne ki RATE. P = W/t = Fv. Jitna jaldi kaam, utni zyada power.
- What is a conservative force? Jis force ke liye closed path par work = 0 (gravity,
  spring). Tab total mechanical energy conserve hoti hai: gain in KE = loss in PE.
- Work-energy theorem. W_total = ΔK — net work = kinetic energy ka change. Ye theorem hi
  har "force → speed" problem ka shortcut hai.
- What is elastic energy in a wire? Wire ko stretch karne par stored energy U = (1/2)F ×
  ΔL. Energy density (per volume) = (1/2) × stress × strain = (1/2)Y(strain)² — bilkul
  spring ke (1/2)kx² jaisa, bas wire ke terms mein.
- Work done pulling a hanging chain. Uniform chain ka kuch hissa table se latka ho to
  use upar kheenchne ka work = (hanging mass) × g × (uske centre of mass ki raise).
  Ek-third length latki ho to W = (m/3) × g × (L/6) = mgL/18.
3. KEY RELATIONS

- W = F·s = Fₓsₓ + Fᵧsᵧ + F₂s₂ (dot product). F = 2i+3j+k, s = i+2j−4k → W = 2+6−4 = 4
  J.
- Spring: U = (1/2)kx²; work from x₁ to x₂ = (1/2)k(x₂² − x₁²).
- Spring 2 cm → 100 J; further 2 cm (total 4 cm) → energy 4× = 400 J → increase 300 J.
  (U ∝ x².)
- Power: P = W/t = Fv.
- Variable force: W = ∫F dx. F = 3ax² − 3 from 0 to 2 → 8a − 6.
- Wire energy: U = (1/2)FΔL = (1/2)Y × (ΔL/L)² × volume. Energy density = (1/2) × stress
  × strain.
- Chain (1/3 length L hanging): W = (m/3) × g × (L/6) = mgL/18.
4. FORMULAS

- W = F s cosθ = F·s ; ΔK = W
- K = (1/2)mv² = p²/2m ; U = mgh ; U_spring = (1/2)kx²
- P = W/t = F v cosθ
- W_spring(x₁→x₂) = (1/2)k(x₂² − x₁²)
- U_wire = (1/2)FΔL ; energy density = (1/2) × stress × strain = (1/2)Y(strain)²
- W_chain = (hanging mass) × g × (height of its centre of mass)
Symbol meanings: F = force, s = displacement, θ = angle between them, k = spring
constant, v = velocity, m = mass, g = 9.8 m/s².
5. CONDITIONS / SPECIAL CASES

- θ = 0 → W = Fs (max). θ = 90° → W = 0 (pushing wall, bag horizontal le jaana). θ =
  180° → W = −Fs (retarding force).
- Conservative field: (p) ME conserved, (q) closed-path work = 0, (r) gain KE = loss PE
  — TEENO correct.
- Spring 1000 N/m, 10 cm → 20 cm: W = 0.5 × 1000 × (0.04 − 0.01) = 15 J.
- Athlete 60 kg, 100 m in 10 s → v ≈ 10 m/s → K = 0.5×60×100 = 3000 J (range 2000–5000
  J).
6. HOW TO RECOGNIZE THE QUESTION

- "Work by force over displacement (vectors)" → dot product W = F·s.
- "Retarding force ka work" → negative.
- "Spring energy / work between two stretches" → W = (1/2)k(x₂² − x₁²).
- "Variable force F(x)" → integrate F dx.
- "Power / kinetic-energy estimate" → P = W/t, K = (1/2)mv².
7. SOLVING METHODS

- Dot-product work: matching components multiply karke add karo (signs dhyan se).
- F(x) force: ∫F dx from x₁ to x₂.
- Estimate problems: rough speed le kar K = (1/2)mv².
8. REAL PYQ EXAMPLES

Q. Calculate the work done by F = 2i + 3j + k on a particle displaced by s = i + 2j − 4k.
   Method: Work = dot product of force and displacement.
   Solve: W = (2)(1) + (3)(2) + (1)(−4) = 2 + 6 − 4 = 4 J.
   Takeaway: Matching components multiply karo; −4k wale sign ka dhyan rakho.

Q. A spring (k = 1000 N/m) is stretched from 10 cm to 20 cm. Work done:
   Method: Spring work between two positions — difference of squares.
   Solve: W = (1/2)k(x₂² − x₁²) = 0.5 × 1000 × (0.04 − 0.01) = 15 J.
   Takeaway: W = (1/2)k(x₂² − x₁²), NOT (1/2)k(x₂ − x₁)².

Q. When a spring is stretched by 2 cm, stored energy is 100 J. Stretched further by 2 cm, energy increases by:
   Method: U ∝ x² — doubling stretch quadruples energy.
   Solve: Total 4 cm → U = 4 × 100 = 400 J. Increase = 400 − 100 = 300 J.
   Takeaway: U ∝ x²: stretch 2× → energy 4×. "Further" → naya minus purana.

Q. When a force retards the motion of a body, the work done is:
   Method: Sign of work — force opposes displacement.
   Solve: θ = 180° → W = Fs cos180° = −Fs → negative.
   Takeaway: Motion oppose karne wala force hamesha negative work karta hai.

Q. Energy stored in a stretched wire is (Y = Young's modulus, S = strain):
   Method: Elastic energy density in a wire.
   Solve: Energy density = (1/2) × stress × strain = (1/2)Y S².
   Takeaway: Wire energy density = (1/2)Y(strain)² — spring ke (1/2)kx² ka wire version.

Q. A uniform chain of length L and mass m lies on a table with one-third of its length hanging. Work to pull it up:
   Method: Work = mgh for the hanging part's centre of mass.
   Solve: W = (m/3) × g × (L/6) = mgL/18.
   Takeaway: Hanging part ka CM L/6 neeche hai — sirf hanging mass par mgh lagao.

9. 10-SECOND TRICKS

- Force opposing motion → work negative (instant).
- Spring stretch 2× → energy 4×.
- Dot product: (2)(1)+(3)(2)+(1)(−4) = 4 — one line.
- Wire energy density = (1/2) × stress × strain.
- Chain pull-up: W = (hanging mass) × g × (CM height).
10. IMPORTANT FACTS

- Work aur energy scalars hain, same dimension [ML²T⁻²].
- Friction hamesha negative work karti hai.
11. COMMON TRAPS

TRAP: Spring work = (1/2)k(x₂ − x₁)².
FIX:  W = (1/2)k(x₂² − x₁²) — difference of squares.
TRAP: Dot product mein −4k component ka sign chhodna.
FIX:  Saare components ke signs rakho: 2+6−4 = 4.
TRAP: Retarding force positive work karti hai.
FIX:  Retarding force ka work NEGATIVE hota hai.
12. GRAPHS / DIAGRAMS

- Force-displacement graph: AREA = work done.
- Spring F vs x: origin se straight line; area = (1/2)kx².
- Power-time graph: area = energy.
13. LAST-MINUTE REVISION

- LAST-MINUTE REVISION
- -W = F s cosθ ; K = (1/2)mv² ; U = (1/2)kx² ; P = W/t = Fv
- -Retarding force → negative work ; θ = 90° → zero work
- -Spring: W = (1/2)k(x₂² − x₁²) ; U ∝ x²

CHAPTER 5 - ROTATIONAL MOTION (SYSTEM OF PARTICLES AND RIGID BODY)
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION

Jab body ghoomti hai (rotation), tab linear quantities ki jagah angular quantities aati
hain: ω (angular velocity), α (angular acceleration), I (moment of inertia), τ (torque),
L (angular momentum). Exam mein sabse zyada marks moment-of-inertia table aur
parallel/perpendicular axis theorem se aate hain.
2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What is moment of inertia? I = rotational inertia = Σmr². Jaise mass linear motion ka
  "resistance" hai, waise I rotation ka resistance hai. Ye body ke mass aur axis se
  mass ki doori (distribution) par depend karta hai — shape aur axis badle to I
  badalta hai.
- What is torque? τ = r × F = rF sinθ = Iα. Torque force aur MOMENT ARM (perpendicular
  distance) par depend karta hai — mass par nahi. Yahi exam ka favourite trap hai.
- What is angular momentum? L = Iω = mvr (particle ke liye). Jab tak external torque na
  ho, L conserved rehta hai. Isi se Kepler ka 2nd law (equal areas) aata hai.
- What is centre of mass? System ka wo point jahan poora mass concentrated maan sakte
  hain. External force na ho to CM ka velocity constant rehta hai.
- Radius of gyration. k = wo distance jahan saara mass rakhne par same I mile: I = Mk².
- CM of uniform bodies. Uniform cube/sphere/rod ka CM GEOMETRIC CENTRE par hota hai —
  cube ka CM body ke centre par (face ya edge par nahi).
- CM formula. x_cm = Σ(mx)/Σm ; V_cm = Σ(mv)/Σm. Do bodies ka system: V_cm = (m₁v₁ +
  m₂v₂)/(m₁+m₂).
- KE of centre of mass. System ki CM motion ki KE = (1/2)(m₁+m₂)V_cm². (Total KE = CM ki
  KE + relative motion ki KE.)
- Angular acceleration in UCM. Uniform circular motion mein ω CONSTANT → angular
  acceleration α = 0.
3. KEY RELATIONS

- L = Iω ; τ = Iα = rF sinθ ; KE_rot = (1/2)Iω².
- Parallel axis: I = I_cm + Md². Perpendicular axis (lamina): I_z = I_x + I_y.
- Ring: MR² (⊥ centre), MR²/2 (diameter), 3MR²/2 (tangent).
- Disc: MR²/2 (⊥ centre), MR²/4 (diameter), 5MR²/4 (tangent in plane).
- Sphere: 2MR²/5 (diameter). Rod: ML²/12 (centre ⊥), ML²/3 (end ⊥).
- Disc diameter = I → ⊥ axis through rim = 6I (I = MR²/4 ⇒ MR² = 4I; rim = 2I + 4I).
- CM: x_cm = Σ(mx)/Σm ; V_cm = Σ(mv)/Σm ; KE_cm = (1/2)(Σm)V_cm².
- τ = r × F (cross product); magnitude rF sinθ; direction right-hand rule se.
- UCM: ω constant → α = 0.
4. FORMULAS

    | Body / Axis | Moment of inertia |
    | Ring, axis ⊥ plane through centre | MR² |
    | Ring, about a diameter | MR²/2 |
    | Ring, about a tangent (in plane) | 3MR²/2 |
    | Disc, axis ⊥ plane through centre | MR²/2 |
    | Disc, about a diameter | MR²/4 |
    | Solid sphere, about diameter | 2MR²/5 |
    | Solid cylinder, about its own axis | MR²/2 |
    | Rod, about centre (⊥) | ML²/12 |
    | Rod, about end (⊥) | ML²/3 |

5. CONDITIONS / SPECIAL CASES

- Torque ∝ force × moment arm (NOT mass, NOT radius alone).
- L conserved → I double ho to ω half (L = Iω). Rotating boy hands chest mein kheenche →
  I ghatta → ω badhta.
- Do bodies mutual gravity se ek dusre ki taraf → V_cm = 0 (no external force). 2 kg at
  20 m/s aur 4 kg at 10 m/s opposite → V_cm = 0.
- Ring tangent = 3MR²/2 (parallel axis se: MR² + MR²/2). 5MR²/2 galat hai.
6. HOW TO RECOGNIZE THE QUESTION

- "MI about a given axis" → table + parallel/perpendicular theorem.
- "Torque kis par depend" → force × moment arm.
- "L conserved (I badla)" → L = Iω.
- "CM velocity of two bodies" → external force zero → V_cm constant.
7. SOLVING METHODS

- MI axis ke hisaab se: parallel/perpendicular theorem lagao.
- Radius of gyration: I = Mk² se k nikalo.
- Angular displacement track par: sweep hua angle padho.
8. REAL PYQ EXAMPLES

Q. What is the moment of inertia of a ring of mass M and radius R about a tangent to the circle?
   Method: Parallel-axis theorem from a diameter.
   Solve: I_tangent = I_diameter + MR² = MR²/2 + MR² = 3MR²/2.
   Takeaway: Ring tangent = 3MR²/2 (5MR²/2 galat hai).

Q. Moment of inertia of a disc about a diameter is I. Its MI about an axis ⊥ to its plane through a point on the rim is:
   Method: Perpendicular-axis + parallel-axis theorem.
   Solve: I⊥centre = I_diameter + I_diameter = 2I. Rim: I = 2I + MR². Disc ke liye MR² = 4I → 2I + 4I = 6I.
   Takeaway: Disc: I_diameter = I → ⊥ axis through centre = 2I, rim = 6I.

Q. Torque depends on:
   Method: Definition of torque.
   Solve: τ = force × moment arm (perpendicular distance).
   Takeaway: Torque = force × moment arm — "mass" wala option galat.

Q. Two bodies of 2 kg (20 m/s) and 4 kg (10 m/s) move toward each other under mutual gravity. Velocity of centre of mass:
   Method: No external force → V_cm constant (initially zero).
   Solve: V_cm = (2×20 + 4×(−10))/6 = 0.
   Takeaway: Mutual attraction = internal force → V_cm nahi badalta.

Q. The centre of mass of a uniform cube is at:
   Method: Uniform body → geometric centre.
   Solve: Cube ka CM uske geometrical centre par hota hai (face/edge/diagonal par nahi).
   Takeaway: Uniform (symmetric) body ka CM = geometric centre.

Q. A ball of mass M (2 m/s) hits a 1 kg ball (1 m/s). KE of centre of mass is 4/3 J. Find M:
   Method: KE_cm = (1/2)(M+1)V_cm² ; V_cm = (2M + 1×1)/(M+1).
   Solve: (1/2)(M+1)[(2M+1)/(M+1)]² = 4/3 → (2M+1)² = (8/3)(M+1).
       3(4M²+4M+1) = 8M+8 → 12M²+4M−5 = 0 → M = 0.5 kg.
   Takeaway: KE_cm = (1/2)(Σm)V_cm² — pehle V_cm nikalo, phir KE.

9. 10-SECOND TRICKS

- Ring tangent = 3MR²/2 ; disc tangent = 5MR²/4 ; sphere = 2MR²/5.
- I double → ω half (L constant).
- Kepler equal areas = angular momentum conservation.
- Uniform body → CM at geometric centre.
- UCM mein α = 0 (ω constant).
10. IMPORTANT FACTS

- Rigid body = fixed shape (particles ke beech distance constant).
- Angular momentum ka dimension = Planck's constant ka dimension = [ML²T⁻¹].
11. COMMON TRAPS

TRAP: Torque "mass" ya "radius" par depend karta hai.
FIX:  Torque = force × moment arm.
TRAP: Ring tangent = 5MR²/2.
FIX:  Ring tangent = 3MR²/2 (parallel axis se: MR²/2 + MR²).
TRAP: Solid sphere ka k = r wale axis ka distance √0.4r hota hai (printed answer).
FIX:  METHOD yaad karo: I = Mk² = Mr² = (2/5)Mr² + Md² → d = r√(3/5). Numbers ki rat mat maro.
12. GRAPHS / DIAGRAMS

- MI axes diagram: ring, disc, sphere, rod — centre/diameter/tangent/end sab draw karke
  yaad karo.
- ω-t graph (constant α): straight line, slope = α.
13. LAST-MINUTE REVISION

- LAST-MINUTE REVISION
- -τ = Iα = rF sinθ ; L = Iω ; KE = (1/2)Iω² ; I = Mk²
- -Ring: MR², MR²/2 (diam), 3MR²/2 (tangent)
- -Disc: MR²/2, MR²/4 (diam), rim-⊥-axis 6I ; Sphere: 2MR²/5 ; Rod: ML²/12, ML²/3

CHAPTER 6 - GRAVITATION
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION

Gravitation batata hai ki masses ek dusre ko kyun kheenchti hain. Is chapter ke teen
pillar: Newton's law, acceleration due to gravity (g), aur escape velocity. Satellite
energy aur Kepler's laws bhi isi se judi hain.
2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What is Newton's law of gravitation? F = GMm/r² — attractive, dono bodies ko jodne
  wali line ke along. G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ (universal constant).
- What is g? Planet ki surface par gravity ka acceleration: g = GM/R². Ye planet ke mass
  aur radius par depend karta hai — aapke mass par nahi.
- What is escape velocity? Wo minimum speed jisse body planet ka gravity chhod kar
  hamesha ke liye nikal jaye: vₑ = √(2GM/R) = √(2gR). Earth ke liye 11.2 km/s.
- What is weightlessness in a satellite? Astronaut aur satellite dono SATH mein free
  fall kar rahe hote hain → relative weight zero. Ye FREE FALL hai, "gravity zero"
  nahi — gravity wahi lagi rehti hai.
- Kepler's laws. (1) elliptical orbit, Sun at focus; (2) equal areas in equal time =
  angular momentum conservation; (3) T² ∝ r³.
3. KEY RELATIONS

- g = GM/R² ; g' at height h (h << R): g' = g(1 − 2h/R).
- g at depth d: g' = g(1 − d/R) — Earth ke centre par zero.
- g = (4/3)πGρR (density form): same radius → g ∝ ρ; same density → g ∝ R.
- vₑ = √(2gR) = √(2GM/R) = 11.2 km/s.
- Planet 4M aur R/2 → g = G(4M)/(R/2)² = 16g.
- Satellite: v = √(GM/r) ; T = 2π√(r³/GM) ; E = −GMm/2r ; PE = 2E ; KE = −E.
- Equal escape velocity → M/R equal (NOT M×R).
4. FORMULAS

- F = GMm/r² ; g = GM/R² ; vₑ = √(2GM/R) = √(2gR)
- Satellite: PE = 2 × E_total ; KE = −E_total ; T = 2π√(r³/GM)
- Kepler: T² ∝ r³ ; areal velocity constant
Symbol meanings: G = universal gravitational constant, M = planet mass, R = planet
radius, r = orbit radius, m = body mass.
5. CONDITIONS / SPECIAL CASES

- g pole se equator tak DECREASE hota hai (rotation + bulge).
- Earth ka radius 1% kam (mass fixed) → g ~2% badhta hai (g = GM/R²).
- 2M aur R/2 wale planet par vₑ double (11.2 → 22.4 km/s).
- Satellite ka time period central mass M aur radius r par depend karta hai, satellite
  ke mass par NAHI.
- Satellite: PE = 2 × total energy. Total energy E₀ → PE = 2E₀, KE = −E₀.
- Radii 1:4, densities 1:2 → g ratio = (ρ₁R₁)/(ρ₂R₂) = (1×1)/(2×4) = 1:8.
- Height par g 1/4 rah jaye → (1−2h/R) = 1/4 → h = 3R/8.
6. HOW TO RECOGNIZE THE QUESTION

- "Planet ka g (M, R diye)" → g ∝ M/R².
- "Escape velocity" → √(2GM/R).
- "Satellite energy / time period" → PE = 2E_total ; T formula.
- "Kepler" → equal areas = angular momentum.
7. SOLVING METHODS

- "Mass k×, radius 1/m×" → g' = k m² × g. Radius factor ko SQUARE karo.
- Equal escape velocity → M/R equal (M×R nahi).
8. REAL PYQ EXAMPLES

Q. If the mass of a planet is four times that of earth and radius is half, gravitational acceleration is:
   Method: g ∝ M/R².
   Solve: g' = 4/(1/2)² = 4 × 4 = 16g.
   Takeaway: Radius factor square karo: (1/2)² = 1/4, to 4/(1/4) = 16.

Q. The escape velocity on earth is 11.2 km/s. On a planet with twice the mass and half the radius, it is:
   Method: vₑ ∝ √(M/R).
   Solve: vₑ' = √(2/(1/2)) = √4 = 2 → 2 × 11.2 = 22.4 km/s.
   Takeaway: vₑ ∝ √(M/R): mass ratio direct, radius ratio ulta, dono ka square root.

Q. A satellite in a circular orbit has total energy E₀. Its potential energy is:
   Method: Satellite energy relation.
   Solve: PE = 2 × E_total = 2E₀ (KE = −E₀).
   Takeaway: Satellite: PE = 2E_total, KE = −E_total — fixed relation.

Q. The escape velocities of planets A and B are equal. Which statement is correct?
   Method: Equal vₑ condition.
   Solve: vₑ = √(2GM/R) equal → M/R equal (M ∝ R), NOT M×R equal.
   Takeaway: Equal escape velocity → M₁/R₁ = M₂/R₂ (product nahi).

9. 10-SECOND TRICKS

- 4M + R/2 → 16g ; 2M + R/2 → vₑ ×2.
- Weightless astronaut = free fall.
- Equal escape velocity → equal M/R.
10. IMPORTANT FACTS

- g ≈ 9.8 m/s²; Moon ≈ 1.63 m/s² (≈ g/6). 10 kg: Earth 98 N, Moon 16.3 N.
- Kepler 2nd law = angular momentum conservation.
11. COMMON TRAPS

TRAP: Equal escape velocity → M₁R₁ = M₂R₂.
FIX:  M₁/R₁ = M₂/R₂ hota hai (M×R galat).
TRAP: Weightless = gravity nahi hai.
FIX:  Weightless = free fall; gravity maujood hai.
TRAP: Pole → equator g badhta hai.
FIX:  g DECREASE hota hai.
12. GRAPHS / DIAGRAMS

- g vs r: Earth ke andar g ∝ r (linear rise); bahar g ∝ 1/r² (fall).
- g vs depth: centre par zero tak linear decrease.
- Orbit: orbital velocity chakkar mein rakhti hai; escape velocity hamesha ke liye chhod
  deti hai.
13. LAST-MINUTE REVISION

- LAST-MINUTE REVISION
- -F = GMm/r² ; g = GM/R² ; vₑ = √(2gR) ; T² ∝ r³
- -4M & R/2 → 16g ; 1% smaller R → g up ~2% ; 2M & R/2 → vₑ ×2

CHAPTER 7 - PROPERTIES OF BULK MATTER (SOLIDS AND FLUIDS)
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION

Ye chapter solids (elasticity) aur fluids (liquids/gases: pressure, surface tension,
viscosity) dono cover karta hai. Har sub-topic se 1-2 fixed questions aate hain: Young's
modulus, excess pressure, terminal velocity, gauge pressure — sab short aur formula-
based hain.
2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What is elasticity? Body par force lagao to wo deform hoti hai. Stress = F/A (force
  per area), strain = ΔL/L (change per original). Young's modulus Y = stress/strain =
  stress-strain curve ke LINEAR part ka slope.
- What is pressure? Force per area. Gauge pressure = absolute − atmospheric = P − Pₐ.
  (Jo gauge padhta hai wo atmospheric ke upar ka extra pressure hai.)
- What is surface tension? Liquid surface ka wo tendency jisse wo apna area minimum
  karti hai — isliye drop spherical hota hai. Ye force/length hai aur temperature
  badhne par DECREASE hoti hai. Soap bubble ki 2 surfaces hoti hain (ΔP = 4T/r), drop
  ki 1 (ΔP = 2T/r).

CHAPTER 8 - THERMODYNAMICS
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION

Thermodynamics = heat, work aur internal energy ka relation. Sirf teen cheezein solidly
yaad karo: first law (ΔQ = ΔU + ΔW), isothermal/adiabatic processes, aur Carnot
efficiency. Ye teeno hi har paper mein aate hain.
2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What is the first law? Energy conservation: ΔQ = ΔU + ΔW. Heat ka kuch hissa internal
  energy badhata hai (ΔU), kuch hissa kaam karta hai (ΔW). Work "on" gas ho to ΔU
  badhta hai, "by" gas ho to ΔU ghat-ta hai.
- What is internal energy? Ideal gas ki U sirf TEMPERATURE par depend karti hai. Isi
  liye isothermal (T constant) mein ΔU = 0.
- Isothermal process. T constant → ΔU = 0 → saari heat work ban jaati hai: Q = W.
- Adiabatic process. Heat exchange nahi (Q = 0) → ΔU = −W. Expansion mein gas kaam karti
  hai → ΔU negative (temperature girta hai). Compression mein ulta.
- What is the second law + entropy? Second law ENTROPY define karta hai (natural
  processes ki direction). Full cycle par ∮(δQ − δW) = 0.
- Carnot engine. Do temperatures ke beech maximum efficiency wala ideal engine: η = 1 −
  T₂/T₁ (temperatures KELVIN mein!).
- Isochoric (constant volume). V constant → koi expansion nahi → work W = 0. Tab ΔQ = ΔU
  (saari heat internal energy ban jaati hai).
- Isobaric (constant pressure). W = PΔV (pressure × volume change).
- Refrigerator. Heat engine ka ULTA — sink se heat nikal kar source ko deta hai.
  Coefficient of performance COP = Q₂/W = T₂/(T₁−T₂) (kelvin mein). Ye engine ki
  efficiency ka reciprocal NAHI hai.
- Polytropic process PVⁿ = constant. n = 1 → isothermal, n = γ → adiabatic. Work done W
  = (P₁V₁ − P₂V₂)/(n − 1).
3. KEY RELATIONS

- ΔQ = ΔU + ΔW ; isothermal: Q = W, ΔU = 0 ; adiabatic: Q = 0, ΔU = −W.
- η = 1 − T₂/T₁ ; η = W/Q₁ (W = ηQ₁). 75% of 300 J → 225 J.
- 127 °C–327 °C → 400 K–600 K → η = 1 − 400/600 = 33.3%.
- 200 J work ON gas (adiabatic) → ΔU = +200 J.
- Isochoric: W = 0, ΔQ = ΔU ; Isobaric: W = PΔV.
- Refrigerator COP = T₂/(T₁ − T₂). 0 °C–100 °C → 273/100 = 2.73.
- PV² = constant → W = (P₁V₁ − P₂V₂)/(2 − 1).
4. FORMULAS

- ΔQ = ΔU + ΔW ; η_carnot = 1 − T₂/T₁ ; η = W/Q₁
- T(K) = t(°C) + 273
- Isochoric: W = 0 ; Isobaric: W = PΔV ; COP_ref = T₂/(T₁−T₂)
- PVⁿ = const → W = (P₁V₁ − P₂V₂)/(n−1)
Symbol meanings: ΔQ = heat supplied, ΔU = internal energy change, ΔW = work by system,
T₁ = source temp, T₂ = sink temp (both kelvin), Q₁ = heat absorbed.
5. CONDITIONS / SPECIAL CASES

- Carnot η = 1/6, sink 62 °C kam → η double: T₁ = 372 K (99 °C), T₂ = 310 K (37 °C).
- Isothermal Q = 100 J → W = 100 J, ΔU = 0.
- Work 333 cal, ΔU 167 cal → Q = 333 + 167 = 500 cal.
- Same 20 K gap: sabse LOW cold/hot ratio jita: 40 K/20 K → η = 50%.
- Source temperature badhao → Carnot efficiency badhti hai.
- Heat aur work PATH functions hain (process par depend karte hain); internal energy
  STATE function hai (process par nahi).
- P-V diagram: area under curve = work; adiabat isotherm se STEEPER (PV^γ).
- Isochoric (V constant) → W = 0 → ΔQ = ΔU.
- Refrigerator 0 °C & 100 °C → COP = 273/(373−273) = 2.73.
- Gas law names: PV = constant (Boyle, T const); V ∝ T (Charles, P const); P ∝ T (Gay-
  Lussac, V const).
6. HOW TO RECOGNIZE THE QUESTION

- "ΔQ, ΔU, W" → first law.
- "Isothermal / adiabatic" → ΔU = 0 / Q = 0.
- "Carnot efficiency" → η = 1 − T₂/T₁ (kelvin!).
- "∮(δQ−δW)" → 0.
7. SOLVING METHODS

- Carnot ke liye hamesha °C → K (add 273).
- "Work ON gas" → ΔU up; "BY gas" → ΔU down.
- η double wale tricks: do equations bana ke solve karo.

2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What is an ideal gas? Bahut saare molecules, negligible volume, elastic collisions,
  koi intermolecular force nahi (sirf collision ke waqt). Real gas mein ye assumptions
  toot-ti hain, isliye van der Waals correction aata hai.

  = √(3k_BT/m). Matlab: v_rms ∝ √T (temperature ka square root) aur v_rms ∝ 1/√M
  (molar mass ka ulta square root). Constant temperature par pressure se INDEPENDENT
  hai.
- Gas law names. Boyle: PV = constant (T const). Charles: V ∝ T (P const). Gay-Lussac: P
  ∝ T (V const). Teeno milkar ideal gas law PV = nRT banate hain.

- v_rms = √(3RT/M) = √(3P/ρ). O₂ (M = 32) at 300 K → 483 m/s; at 1200 K → 483 × 2 = 966
  m/s.

- Boyle: PV const; Charles: V ∝ T; Gay-Lussac: P ∝ T.

1. INTRODUCTION

Ye chapter do cheezein cover karta hai: SHM (simple harmonic motion) aur waves
(sound/mechanical vs EM). SHM ke questions fixed pattern ke hain — velocity at x = A/2,
amax/vmax ratio, energy ∝ A².
2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What is SHM? Wo motion jisme restoring force displacement ke ∝ aur opposite hota hai:
  F = −kx. Mean position ke around back-and-forth oscillation.
- Mean vs extreme. Mean position par velocity MAXIMUM aur acceleration ZERO. Extreme par
  velocity zero aur acceleration MAXIMUM. Ye hi sabse common question hai.
- SHM velocity. v = ω√(A² − x²). Mean (x=0) par v = vmax = ωA; extreme (x=A) par v = 0.
- What is a wave? Energy ka ek jagah se dusri jagah transfer, bina matter transfer ke.
  Mechanical waves (sound) ko medium chahiye; EM waves ko nahi.
- Sound. Mechanical wave; speed solids > liquids > gases. Bats ultrasonic waves
  (reflection) se obstacles detect karte hain.
- What is a wave number? k = 2π/λ — 2π length mein kitni waves (propagation constant bhi
  kehte hain). y = A sin(ωt − kx) mein x ka coefficient hi k hai.
- What is a stationary wave? Do SAME waves opposite directions mein milti hain to
  stationary wave banti hai. Isme ENERGY TRANSFER NAHI hota (energy nodes ke beech
  trapped). Nodes = zero displacement, antinodes = maximum.
- What is intensity of a wave? Intensity ∝ (amplitude)² — amplitude double → intensity
  4×.
- SHM phase relations. x = A sin ωt. Velocity v = dx/dt leads x by 90°; acceleration a =
  −ω²x is 180° out of phase with x (a leads v by 90°). Mean par v max; extreme par a
  max.
- Phase difference of two waves. y = A sin(ωt + φ₁) aur y = A sin(ωt + φ₂) → phase
  difference = |φ₁ − φ₂|.
3. KEY RELATIONS

- v = ω√(A² − x²) ; vmax = ωA = 2πA/T.
- x = A/2 par: v = vmax√(3/4) = (√3/2)vmax.
- Ratio amax/vmax = ω = 2πf.
- a = ω²x → ω = √(a/x) = √(2/0.02) = 10 rad/s.
- SHM energy E ∝ A² (A double → KE ×4).
- k = 2π/λ ; v = ω/k ; particle velocity in wave: v_max = Aω.
- I ∝ A² ; stationary wave → energy transfer ZERO.
- SHM: v leads x by 90°; a leads v by 90° (a = −ω²x).
- Time to reach x from mean: t = (T/2π) × sin⁻¹(x/A). Mean → A/2: t = T/12 (fixed
  value).
- Two waves: Δφ = |φ₁ − φ₂| (π/3 − π/4 = π/12).
4. FORMULAS

- T = 2π√(m/k) (spring) ; T = 2π√(L/g) (pendulum)
- vmax = ωA ; amax = ω²A ; v = ω√(A²−x²) ; E = (1/2)mω²A²
- k = 2π/λ ; v = ω/k ; I ∝ A²
- Particle velocity in wave: v_max = Aω
Symbol meanings: A = amplitude, ω = angular frequency, x = displacement from mean, T =
time period, f = frequency, m = mass, k = spring constant, L = pendulum length.

CHAPTER 11 - ELECTROSTATICS

1. INTRODUCTION

Electrostatics = rest par charges ka study. Idhar chaar fixed areas hain: Coulomb's
force, electric field/potential, dipole, aur capacitors. Sab formula-based hain aur
ratios se turant solve hote hain.
2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What does a charge at rest produce? Sirf ELECTRIC field (magnetic field nahi).
  Magnetic field sirf MOVING charge banata hai. Ye direct fact-question hai.
- What is Coulomb's law? F = k q₁q₂/r² (k = 9×10⁹). Medium (dielectric) mein force ghat
  jaati hai: F' = F/K = F/εᵣ.
- What is electric field? E = F/q = kq/r² (point charge). Parallel plates ke beech
  uniform field E = V/d.
- What is potential? V = kq/r. Conductors ke ANDAR field zero aur potential constant
  hota hai.
- What is a dipole? +q aur −q ka pair, separation d. Dipole moment p = qd. Field mein
  dipole par torque τ = pE sinθ.
- What is a capacitor? Charge store karne wala device. C = Q/V = ε₀A/d. Energy U =
  (1/2)CV² = Q²/2C.
- What is potential difference from work? V = W/q — charge q ko move karne mein jo work
  W karna pade, wo potential difference se aata hai. Equipotential surface (same V)
  par move karne mein work = 0.
- What is surface charge density? σ = Q/A — charge per area. Spherical shell: σ =
  Q/(4πR²). Thin shell ke just bahar E = σ/ε₀.
- What is charge quantisation? Charge discrete hota hai: q = ne (n = integer, e =
  1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C). e ka koi fraction possible nahi.
- How does a dipole's field vary with r? Axial aur equatorial dono par E ∝ 1/r³;
  potential V ∝ 1/r². (Point charge ke E ∝ 1/r² se zyada jaldi girta hai.)
- PE of a charge system. Do charges ki PE = kq₁q₂/r. Teen charges equilateral triangle
  (side x) par system ki U = 3kQ²/x — aur yehi work hai system arrange karne mein.
3. KEY RELATIONS

- F = kq₁q₂/r² ; E = F/q = kq/r² ; E(plates) = V/d.
- Field ratio: E₂/E₁ = (q₂/q₁)(r₁/r₂)². 2 μC at r vs 8 μC at 2r → (8/2)(1/2)² = 1.
- C = ε₀A/d ; U = (1/2)CV² = Q²/2C.
- Series: 1/C = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ ; Parallel: C = C₁ + C₂.
- Dipole SHM: ω = √(2qE/md).
- Gauss: ∮E·dA = q/ε₀ (electricity) ; ∮B·dA = 0 (magnetism).
- V = W/q ; equipotential par W = 0.
- σ = Q/A = Q/4πR² (shell) ; E(shell outside) = σ/ε₀.
- q = ne (quantisation); e = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C.
- Conductor ka saara charge OUTER surface par rehta hai (E inside = 0) — external field
  mein bhi redistribution surface par hi hoti hai.
- Do charges ke beech CONDUCTOR plate rakho → net force ZERO ho jaata hai (conductor ke
  andar E = 0).
- Dipole: E ∝ 1/r³, V ∝ 1/r². System PE: U = kq₁q₂/r.
4. FORMULAS

- F = (1/4πε₀) q₁q₂/r² ; E = F/q = kq/r² ; E = V/d
- τ = pE sinθ (p = qd) ; dipole SHM ω = √(2qE/md)
- C = Q/V = ε₀A/d ; U = (1/2)CV² = Q²/2C
- V = W/q ; σ = Q/A ; E = σ/ε₀ (just outside conductor)
- U_system = k q₁q₂/r ; dipole: E ∝ 1/r³, V ∝ 1/r²
Symbol meanings: q = charge, r = distance, k = 9×10⁹ Nm²/C², E = field, V = potential, p
= dipole moment, C = capacitance, ε₀ = permittivity, A = plate area, d = separation.
5. CONDITIONS / SPECIAL CASES

- Dielectric K=2 → force F/2.
- Charged spherical conductor ke andar: E = 0, V constant.
- Plates −10 V aur +30 V, 2 cm → E = 40/0.02 = 2000 V/m.
- 3 × 4 μF → 6 μF: do series (2 μF) + ek parallel.

  → 5 μF).
- −5 C ko 100 J mein move karo → V = W/q = 100/5 = 20 V.
- Equipotential (5 V) par 10 C move → W = qΔV = 10 × 0 = 0.
- Shell σ = 88.54 C/m², 12 mm bahar → E = σ/ε₀ = 88.54/8.85×10⁻¹² ≈ 10¹³ N/C.
- 3 charges +Q triangle side x → U = 3kQ²/x.

CHAPTER 12 - CURRENT ELECTRICITY
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION

Current electricity = moving charges. Ohm's law, resistance, series/parallel,
Kirchhoff's laws aur power — ye sab hi exam ke core hain. Fixed numericals hain jo ratio
method se turant solve hote hain.
2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What is current? I = Q/t — charge ke flow ki rate. Conventional current positive
  charge ki direction mein maana jaata hai. Wire current flow ke waqt NEUTRAL rehti
  hai (electrons aate-jaate hain, charge balance rehta hai).
- What is Ohm's law? V = IR. Resistance R = ρL/A — material (ρ), length aur area par
  depend karta hai.
- What are Kirchhoff's laws? KCL (current law) = CHARGE conservation (junction par
  current in = out). KVL (voltage law) = ENERGY conservation (loop mein voltage sum =
  0).
- What is power? P = VI = I²R = V²/R. AC mein Pavg = Vrms Irms cosφ.
- What is internal resistance? Battery ke andar ki khud ki resistance r. Circuit mein
  current I = E/(R + r). Terminal voltage V = E − Ir (current draw karne par EMF se
  kam).
- What is drift velocity? Electrons ka average slow net motion (electric field ke
  against): I = neAv_d. n = electrons/m³, e = charge, A = area, v_d = drift velocity.
  I aur A dono double → v_d SAME (2/2 = 1).
- What is conductance? Resistance ka reciprocal: G = 1/R, unit siemens (S).
- What is a temperature coefficient? R = R₀(1 + αΔT). Metals ka α positive (R badhta
  hai), semiconductors ka α NEGATIVE (R ghatta hai).
3. KEY RELATIONS

- R = ρL/A : length aur area DONO double → R SAME (2/2 = 1).
- ρ₁ = 2×10⁻⁸, ρ₂ = 6×10⁻⁸ (same L, A) → R₁/R₂ = 1:3.
- Parallel (2 resistors): product/sum → 10 kΩ ∥ 30 kΩ = 7.5 kΩ.
- 3 equal R parallel + R series → 4R/3.
- Same battery: E = I₁R₁ = I₂R₂.
- p(t) = v(t)·i(t) ; w(t) = ∫p(t)dt.
- I = E/(R+r) ; V_terminal = E − Ir. (E = 4 V, r = 2 Ω, R = 7 Ω → I = 4/9 A.)
- I = neAv_d → v_d ∝ I/A. I aur A dono double → v_d unchanged.
- EMF cell ke ELECTRODES/electrolyte par depend karta hai — circuit (R, I) par nahi.
- R aur V se length: R = ρL/A, V = AL → L = √(RV/ρ).
4. FORMULAS

- V = IR ; R = ρL/A ; Rs = R₁ + R₂ ; 1/Rp = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂
- P = VI = I²R = V²/R ; Pavg = Vrms Irms cosφ
- I = E/(R+r) ; V = E − Ir ; G = 1/R
- I = neAv_d ; R = R₀(1 + αΔT)
Symbol meanings: I = current, V = voltage, R = resistance, ρ = resistivity, L = length,
A = cross-sectional area, P = power.
5. CONDITIONS / SPECIAL CASES

- L double → R double; A double → R half; dono → no change.
- Semiconductor (germanium) mein temperature badhne par R GHATTA hai.
- V = 100 sin100t, I = 100 sin(100t+π/3) mA → Pavg = (100/√2)(0.1/√2)cos60° = 2.5 W.
- Resistivity material property hai; resistance shape par depend karta hai.
- Battery 4 V, r = 2 Ω, R = 7 Ω → I = 4/(7+2) = 4/9 A.
- I aur A dono double → v_d SAME (I/A ratio constant).
- Heater wire: nichrome (high resistivity + high melting point).
- n cells parallel (har cell r) → total internal resistance r/n.
6. HOW TO RECOGNIZE THE QUESTION

- "R = ρL/A (L, A badle)" → ratio method.
- "Series/parallel" → product/sum (do resistors).
- "KCL vs KVL" → charge vs energy.
- "Same battery, two currents" → E equate karo.
- "Battery + internal resistance" → I = E/(R+r).
- "Drift velocity vs I, A" → I = neAv_d.
7. SOLVING METHODS

- Do resistors parallel: product/sum.
- Same-battery problems: E = I₁R₁ = I₂R₂.

2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What does the eye see? Retina par image REAL, INVERTED, DIMINISHED.
- What is dispersion? White light prism se guzarti hai to alag-alag colours mein toot
  jaati hai (alag λ → alag μ → alag deviation). VIOLET sabse zyada deviate hota hai (μ

  MAXIMUM.

  rainbow banate hain (red bahar, violet andar).
- What is lens power? P = 1/f (f metre mein), unit DIOPTRE (D). Convex lens f +ve → P
  +ve; concave → P −ve. +2.5 D → f = 1/2.5 = 0.4 m = 40 cm convex.
- What is the lens-maker formula? 1/f = (μ−1)(1/R₁ − 1/R₂). Sign convention: convex
  surface R +ve, concave R −ve. Lens ko liquid (μ_l) mein duba do → μ → μ_g/μ_l.
- Mirrors inclined. Do plane mirrors θ angle par → number of images N = 360/θ − 1 (jab
  360/θ even integer ho).

  2i (i = 30° → δ = 120°).
- What is Huygens' principle? Wavefront ka HAR point naya secondary wavelet banata hai;
  unka envelope agla wavefront hai. Isse reflection AUR refraction dono explain hote
  hain.
- What is polarisation? Sirf TRANSVERSE waves polarise hoti hain (light haan, sound
  nahi). Reflection se light partially polarise hoti hai; Brewster angle par puri: tan
  θ_B = μ.
- Diffraction condition. Jab slit/obstacle ka size wavelength ke barabar ho tab
  diffraction clearly dikhta hai (corona around moon = diffraction).
- Colour of objects. Object apna colour usi wavelength ko reflect karke dikhata hai. Red
  paper yellow light mein → BLACK (yellow absorb ho jaata hai, kuch reflect nahi
  hota).
3. KEY RELATIONS

CHAPTER 17 - DUAL NATURE OF MATTER AND RADIATION

1. INTRODUCTION

Ye chapter batata hai ki light wave BHI hai aur particle BHI, aur moving particle ki
apni ek wave bhi hoti hai. Fixed patterns: photon energy (12400/λ shortcut), work
function, aur de Broglie wavelength.
2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What is dual nature? Light wave ki tarah behave karti hai (interference, diffraction)
  aur particle ki tarah bhi (photon). Photon energy E = hν = hc/λ.
- What is the photoelectric effect? Light metal par girti hai to electrons nikalte hain
  — lekin TABHI jab ν > threshold frequency ν₀ (minimum FREQUENCY, intensity nahi).
- What is work function? φ = hν₀ = hc/λ₀ — electron nikaalne ke liye minimum energy.
- What is Einstein's equation? hν = φ + K_max. Photon ki energy = work function + max
  kinetic energy.
- What are matter waves? Moving particle ki de Broglie wavelength λ = h/p = h/√(2mK).
3. KEY RELATIONS

- E = hc/λ ; E(eV) = 12400/λ(Å) = 1240/λ(nm).
- 1 eV = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ J (joules mein convert karne ke liye eV ko 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ se multiply
  karo).
- λ = 6000 Å → 2.06 eV ; λ₀ = 5000 Å (sodium) → φ ≈ 4×10⁻¹⁹ J.
- de Broglie: λ = h/p = h/√(2mK) ; λ = 0.2 Å → p = 3.3×10⁻²³ kg m/s.
- Same KE: λ ∝ 1/√m → λₚ/λₑ = √(mₑ/mₚ) ≈ 1/43.
4. FORMULAS

- E = hν = hc/λ ; λ = h/p = h/√(2mK)
- φ = hν₀ = hc/λ₀ ; hν = φ + K_max
- E(eV) = 1240/λ(nm) = 12400/λ(Å)
Symbol meanings: h = Planck's constant, ν = frequency, ν₀ = threshold frequency, λ =
wavelength, λ₀ = threshold wavelength, φ = work function, K_max = max kinetic energy, p
= momentum, m = mass.
5. CONDITIONS / SPECIAL CASES

- Intensity → electrons ki NUMBER (current); frequency → emission + KE.
- Photoelectric emission instantaneous hoti hai.
- Threshold = minimum FREQUENCY (not intensity, not power).
6. HOW TO RECOGNIZE THE QUESTION

- "Photon energy from λ" → 12400/λ(Å).
- "Work function from λ₀" → hc/λ₀.
- "Threshold" → frequency.
- "de Broglie" → λ = h/p = h/√(2mK).
7. SOLVING METHODS

- E(eV) = 12400/λ(Å) one step.
- Same-KE de Broglie: λ ∝ 1/√m.
8. REAL PYQ EXAMPLES

Q. If the wavelength of a photon is 6000 Å, its energy will be:
   Method: E(eV) = 12400/λ(Å).
   Solve: E = 12400/6000 = 2.06 eV.
   Takeaway: 12400/λ(Å) — eV nikalne ka one-step shortcut.

Q. An electron and a proton have the same kinetic energy. The ratio of their de Broglie wavelengths is:
   Method: λ ∝ 1/√m (same KE).
   Solve: λₚ/λₑ = √(mₑ/mₚ) ≈ 1/43.
   Takeaway: Halka particle → lambi λ. Same KE → λ ∝ 1/√m.

   Method: λ = h/p.
   Solve: p = h/λ = 6.6×10⁻³⁴ / 0.2×10⁻¹⁰ = 3.3×10⁻²³ kg m/s.
   Takeaway: p = h/λ — direct division.

   Takeaway: Emission ka gate FREQUENCY hai; intensity sirf current badhati hai.

Q. If the threshold wavelength for sodium is 5000 Å, its work function is:
   Method: φ = hc/λ₀.
   Solve: φ = 6.6×10⁻³⁴ × 3×10⁸ / 5×10⁻⁷ ≈ 4×10⁻¹⁹ J.
   Takeaway: φ = hc/λ₀ — threshold wavelength se work function.

9. 10-SECOND TRICKS

- 12400/λ(Å) = eV.

10. IMPORTANT FACTS

- Photon: E = hν. Matter wave: λ = h/p.
11. COMMON TRAPS

FIX:  Frequency threshold se upar honi chahiye; intensity sirf current badhati hai.
TRAP: Work function eV aur J mix karna.
FIX:  Units consistent rakho — 12400/λ(Å) eV deta hai.

- Stopping potential vs ν: same intercept wali line.

CHAPTER 19 - ELECTRONIC DEVICES
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION

Ye chapter semiconductors, diodes, transistors aur logic gates cover karta hai. Fixed
facts: doping (N vs P type), Zener diode, rectifier chain, aur depletion region.
2. CORE CONCEPTS

- What is doping? Pure semiconductor mein impurity milana. Pentavalent (P, As, Sb) →
  N-TYPE (extra electrons). Trivalent (B, Ga, In) → P-TYPE (holes).
- What is a p-n junction? P aur N ke beech depletion region banta hai. Ye REVERSE bias
  mein WIDEN hota hai, forward bias mein narrow.
- What is a Zener diode? HEAVILY doped diode jo REVERSE bias mein operate karta hai —
  VOLTAGE REGULATOR ke roop mein.
- Why npn over pnp? Electron mobility > hole mobility, isliye npn faster.
- What is rectification? AC → DC. Chain: Rectifier → Filter (smooth) → Regulator
  (constant).
- What are logic gates? Digital circuits ke basic blocks. AND (output 1 sirf jab dono
  inputs 1), OR (koi ek 1 to output 1), NOT (invert), NAND (AND ka ulta), NOR (OR ka
  ulta), XOR (inputs alag to 1). Truth table se koi bhi combination nikal jaata hai.
- What are energy bands? Conduction band = wo band jisme electrons freely conduct karte
  hain. Valence band = atoms se bandhe electrons. Conductor mein dono overlap;
  insulator mein bada gap.
- Ge at 0 K. Absolute zero par germanium INSULATOR jaisa behave karta hai (koi free
  electron nahi).
- Emitter. Transistor ka emitter HEAVILY doped aur THIN hota hai (zyada carriers inject
  karne ke liye).
3. KEY RELATIONS

- N-type: pentavalent impurity; P-type: trivalent impurity.
- Zener: heavily doped, reverse bias, voltage regulator.
- npn > pnp (electron mobility).
- AND: Y = A·B ; OR: Y = A+B ; NOT: Y = Ā ; NAND = AND + NOT ; NOR = OR + NOT.
- AND + NOT ka combination = NAND gate.
- Conduction band electrons hi conduction mein hissa lete hain.
- Ge at 0 K = insulator; emitter heavily doped + thin.
4. FORMULAS

- Diode: forward bias mein conduct; Zener reverse breakdown mein conduct
- β (current gain) = Ic/Ib (common emitter)
- AND: Y = A·B ; OR: Y = A+B ; NOT: Y = Ā ; NAND = NOT(A·B) ; NOR = NOT(A+B)
5. CONDITIONS / SPECIAL CASES

- Ga doped germanium → P-type; P/Sb/Bi doped → N-type.
- Reverse bias only → depletion width badhti hai.
- AC→DC order: rectifier → filter → regulator.
6. HOW TO RECOGNIZE THE QUESTION

- "N vs P type" → pentavalent vs trivalent.
- "Zener" → heavily doped, reverse, regulator.
- "Depletion width" → reverse bias.
- "AC→DC chain" → rectifier → filter → regulator.
7. SOLVING METHODS

- Doping type: periodic group (III vs V).
- AC→DC order yaad rakho.
8. REAL PYQ EXAMPLES

   Method: Doping type by impurity group.

   Solve: Pentavalent impurity (phosphorus/arsenic).
   Takeaway: Pentavalent → N-type; trivalent → P-type.

Q. Zener diode is ____ doped and connected in ____ bias, used as a ____:
   Method: Zener diode properties.
   Solve: Heavily doped, reverse bias, voltage regulator.
   Takeaway: Zener = heavily doped + reverse + regulator.

Q. The increase in the width of the depletion region in a p-n junction is due to:
   Method: Depletion vs bias.
   Solve: Reverse bias only.
   Takeaway: Reverse bias widens depletion; forward narrows it.

Q. Constant DC from variable AC. Correct order of operations:
   Method: AC→DC chain.
   Solve: Rectifier → filter → regulator.
   Takeaway: Rectifier (AC→DC) → filter (smooth) → regulator (constant).

Q. n-p-n transistors are preferred over p-n-p because:
   Method: Transistor mobility.
   Solve: Electron mobility > hole mobility.
   Takeaway: npn > pnp — electrons faster.

Q. Which logic gate gives output Y = 1 only when both inputs are 1?
   Method: AND gate definition.
   Solve: AND gate (Y = A·B).
   Takeaway: AND = dono 1 tab 1; OR = koi ek 1 tab 1.

Q. AND gate followed by NOT gate gives:
   Method: Gate combination.
   Solve: AND + NOT = NAND gate.
   Takeaway: AND + NOT = NAND; OR + NOT = NOR.

Q. Which band in a solid has electrons that participate in conduction?
   Method: Energy bands.
   Solve: Conduction band (valence band ke electrons bandhe hote hain).
   Takeaway: Conduction = conduction band electrons.

Q. Pure germanium at absolute zero behaves as:
   Method: Semiconductor at 0 K.
   Solve: Insulator (koi free electron nahi).
   Takeaway: 0 K par semiconductor = insulator.

- Zener = reverse-biased regulator.
